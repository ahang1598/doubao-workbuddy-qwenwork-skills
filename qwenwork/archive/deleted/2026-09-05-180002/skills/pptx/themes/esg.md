# Theme — ESG / Sustainability

**Voice**: optimistic; organic; calm.
**Fit**: ESG reports, sustainability commitments, green-tech, NGO
storytelling, mission-driven announcements.
**Don't fit**: aggressive sales decks, financial dashboards,
hard-selling product launches.

## Palette

| Slot | Hex | Name |

| primary | `#1F4D3A` | forest |
| secondary | `#7FB069` | moss |

| muted | `#8A8278` | stone |
| bg | `#F5F1E8` | cream |
| on_bg | `#1F4D3A` | forest |

## Fonts

- header: `Source Serif 4`
- body: `Inter`

## image_style suffix

> `natural light photography, plants / people / landscape, warm earth
> tones, no text, no logos`

## Grammar

- **Cover**: forest panel + cream type.
- **Body**: cream bg + photo half-spreads + forest hairlines.
- **KPI**: moss-green positive deltas only — **no red.** The deck is
  optimistic by design; even cautionary numbers should read as
  forward motion (e.g., progress toward a goal, not regression
  alarm).
- **Closer**: full-bleed landscape photo.

## Forbid

- Aggressive accents (no soft red, no orange alarm tones)
- Hard right angles in *photos* (rounded image frames are fine here —
  this archetype softens the typical pptx-0630 default)
