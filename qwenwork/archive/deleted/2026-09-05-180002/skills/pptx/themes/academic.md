# Theme — Academic / Research

**Voice**: rigorous; dense; citation-friendly.
**Fit**: academic talks, research summaries, technical white papers,
conference posters, internal research reviews.
**Don't fit**: marketing, lifestyle, executive summaries, anything
that wants to feel "designed."

## Palette

|---|---|---|
| primary | `#111827` | ink |
| secondary | `#9B2226` | brick (figure refs / emphasis) |
| accent | `#9B2226` | brick |
| muted | `#6B7280` | gray |
| bg | `#FFFFFF` | paper |
| on_bg | `#111827` | ink |

## Fonts

- header: `Source Serif 4`
- body: `Source Serif 4`

(Single serif face; consistency over hierarchy via weight + size.)

## image_style suffix

Not used — this archetype prefers diagrams to photos. Skip the image
fetch step entirely; the deck reads better without stock photography.

## Grammar

- White bg + thin black hairlines + dense small-cap section labels.
- Two-column body, figures numbered (`Fig. 1`, `Fig. 2`, …).
- Tables instead of KPI cards — academic audiences read tables better
  than oversized stat callouts.
- References / citations in `0.7em` muted gray at slide bottom.

## Forbid

- Rounded corners
- Gradients
- Emoji
- Cartoonish illustrations
- KPI cards (use tables)
