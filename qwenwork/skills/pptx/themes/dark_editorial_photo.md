# Theme — Dark Editorial Photo

**Voice**: cinematic; full-bleed photo; champagne hairline.
**Fit**: dark / cinematic launches, luxury, photo essays, premium
collector content, fashion editorials.
**Don't fit**: anything that won't have strong photography to drive
it (data-dense reports, tabular summaries, classical content).

## Palette

| Slot | Hex | Name |
|---|---|---|
| primary | `#0C0B0A` | near-black (also bg) |
| secondary | `#BE9C54` | champagne |
| accent | `#F4EFE6` | ivory |
| muted | `#8A8278` | warm gray |
| bg | `#0C0B0A` | near-black |
| on_bg | `#F4EFE6` | ivory |

## Fonts

- header: `Playfair Display`
- body: `Inter`

## image_style suffix

> `cinematic editorial photograph, low-key lighting, deep shadows,
> champagne highlights, luxury subject, no text, no logos`

## Grammar

Full dark bg + champagne hairline frame inset `0.35″`.

- **Cover**: full-bleed photo + dark gradient overlay + ivory serif
  title.
- **Section dividers**: half-bleed photo + giant serif numeral.
- **Closer**: near-black bg + italic serif sign-off + champagne meta
  line.

## Forbid

- Rounded corners
- Shadows
- Gradients (the champagne hairline carries the whole frame)
