# Theme — Swiss

Two paired neo-grotesque flavors that share grammar — only the palette
differs. Pick one based on tone (yellow = energetic / poster, IKB =
serious / institutional).

**Voice**: neo-grotesque; type-driven; strict grid; aggressive scale.
**Fit**: design-y content, gallery / exhibition catalogs, poster
collections, brand books, manifestos, type-led announcements.
**Don't fit**: financial decks, classical content, anything that
needs warmth or photography to carry it.

## Fonts (both)

- header: `Inter` heavy / black
- body: `Inter`

## image_style suffix (both, if used at all)

> `flat graphic, single subject on solid background, no text overlay,
> no decoration`

## Grammar (both)

- Aggressive title sizes — `100–200pt`. Display type *is* the design.
- Tight typographic grid; align everything to it.
- Huge negative space between elements.
- One or two punctuation glyphs as design elements (slash, asterisk,
  arrow) at large scale.
- Section labels in `ALL-CAPS` with wide tracking (still ≤ 200 spc).
- No photos unless the photo is the entire slide (no inset frames).

## Forbid

- Rounded corners
- Shadows
- Gradients
- Serif fonts
- Cute illustrations / icon clutter
- Decorative cards or panels
