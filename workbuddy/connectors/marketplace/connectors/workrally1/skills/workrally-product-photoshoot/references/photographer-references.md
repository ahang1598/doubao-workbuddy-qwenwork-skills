# Photographer References

Use 2–3 matching commercial-photography entries as internal craft anchors. Pick
entries matched to the mode and preset, then extract only their one-line visual
descriptors into the `[STYLE REFERENCE]` prompt section.

## How to use

Never put a photographer name, publication, retailer, or third-party brand into
the generation prompt. Combine the adjacent descriptor language instead: color
handling, staging, light, surface, mood, and photographic register. Names in
this file are lookup keys only and must not appear in questions or user-facing
output.

## Combination guidance

Pick photographers that share a sensibility — don't combine someone like Tim Walker (surreal whimsy) with Peter Hurley (corporate clean). They pull in opposite directions.

**Strong combinations:**
- Carl Kleiner + Aaron Tilley + Bobby Doherty (clean editorial product)
- Sølve Sundsbø + Mert & Marcus + David Sims (glossy beauty)
- Linda Pugliese + Aubrie Pick + Bobbi Lin (warm lifestyle food)
- Tim Walker + Mert & Marcus + Steven Meisel (high editorial fashion)
- Beth Kirby + Brittany Ambridge + Pia Ulin (warm hand-crafted lifestyle)
- Annie Leibovitz + Mark Seliger + Peter Lindbergh (editorial portrait)
- Spencer Lowell + Aaron Tilley + Joe Pugliese (tech-modern editorial)

**Weak combinations to avoid:**
- Mixing surreal with clean
- Mixing high-fashion with corporate
- Mixing dark moody with bright lifestyle
