# Seasonal & Peak Calendar Notes for FBA Inventory Planning

## US Peak Storage Window

- **October – December**: Storage rates roughly triple for standard-size items ($0.78 → $2.40 / cu ft).
- Additional peak fulfillment surcharges commonly apply mid-October through mid-January.
- Goal: Arrive inventory in Jan–Sep when possible; avoid large inbound arrivals in late September if sell-through will leave units sitting into Q4.

## Typical Category Seasonality (US)

High-level patterns (adjust with your own 12–24 month data):

- **Q4 (Oct–Dec)**: Toys, electronics, home décor, apparel, gifts — major demand spike. Inventory must be in FC by early–mid October.
- **January–February**: Post-holiday clearance / returns; many categories soft. Good window to clear aged stock.
- **Spring (Mar–May)**: Outdoor, garden, spring apparel, home improvement ramp.
- **Summer (Jun–Aug)**: Outdoor living, travel, seasonal sports, back-to-school prep.
- **Prime Day / Big Deal events**: Usually July and October — temporary velocity spikes of 2–5× on promoted ASINs. Pre-position inventory 3–6 weeks ahead.
- **Back-to-School**: July–September for relevant categories.

## Planning Rules of Thumb

1. For highly seasonal items, build inventory for the peak period and plan aggressive sell-through or removal before aged thresholds (181 days).
2. Non-seasonal / staple items: keep steady 30–45 day cover year-round; use Q4 only for true demand uplift, not speculative overstock.
3. When calculating cash for Q4 inbound, include the higher storage rate in the cost model for any units expected to remain after peak.
4. Removal / liquidation decisions should be evaluated against the aged surcharge schedule — paying removal fees is often cheaper than 271+ day surcharges.

## Other Regions

- UK & EU: Similar Q4 peak storage uplift (roughly doubles). Local holidays (Black Friday, Christmas, Singles Day influence, local sales events) shift timing slightly.
- Japan: Golden Week, year-end/New Year, and specific seasonal categories (e.g., summer goods) dominate; check local Seller Central calendars.
