# Amazon FBA Fees Reference (2026)

Primary focus: **US marketplace**. Other regions noted at the end. Always confirm latest rates in Seller Central — fees change.

## US Monthly Storage Fees (2026)

| Size Tier              | Jan–Sep (Off-Peak) | Oct–Dec (Peak) |
|------------------------|--------------------|----------------|
| Standard-size          | $0.78 / cu ft     | $2.40 / cu ft |
| Oversize               | $0.56 / cu ft     | $1.40 / cu ft |
| Dangerous Goods (std)  | higher            | higher        |

Charged on average daily inventory volume. Assessed around the 15th of each month.

## US Aged Inventory Surcharge (on top of monthly storage)

Effective from ~181 days. Rates (per cu ft per month):

| Inventory Age     | Surcharge          |
|-------------------|--------------------|
| 181–210 days      | $0.50             |
| 211–240 days      | $1.00             |
| 241–270 days      | $1.50             |
| 271–300 days      | $5.45             |
| 301–330 days      | $5.70             |
| 331–365 days      | $5.90             |
| 366–455 days      | $6.90 or $0.30/unit (whichever greater) |
| 456+ days         | $7.90 or $0.35/unit (whichever greater) |

**Key planning rule**: Prefer more frequent shipments over letting inventory age past 180 days. The surcharge escalates sharply after 270 days.

## US Storage Utilization Surcharge

Applies to Professional sellers when average weeks of supply exceed thresholds (roughly >22 weeks). Additional per-cu-ft charge on top of base storage. Higher weeks of supply = higher surcharge. Keep most SKUs under ~45–60 days of cover where possible.

## Low-Inventory-Level Fee

Amazon charges extra when inventory is too low relative to demand (roughly <28 days of cover on some ASINs). Balance against aged-inventory risk — sweet spot for many sellers in 2026 is **30–45 days of cover** on stable SKUs.

## Peak Season Notes (US)

- Storage rates jump significantly Oct–Dec.
- Additional peak fulfillment surcharges often apply mid-Oct to mid-Jan.
- Plan inbound arrivals for Jan–Sep whenever demand allows.

## UK & EU Storage Utilisation Surcharge

Applies to Professional sellers when storage utilisation ratio (avg daily inventory volume ÷ avg daily shipped volume over ~13 weeks) exceeds **22 weeks**.

- Does not apply to inventory <30 days old, Clothing/Eyewear/Shoes/Bags categories, new sellers, or very low volume.
- Surcharge is added on top of the base monthly storage fee and rises with higher weeks-of-supply ratios (examples for UK standard: +£0.42 at 22–28 weeks up to +£2.29 above 52 weeks).

Keep most SKUs well under 22 weeks of supply where possible.

## Peak Season Notes (UK & EU)

- Storage rates roughly double in Oct–Dec.
- From **15 Oct 2026 – 14 Jan 2027**, additional peak fulfilment surcharges apply (UK ~£0.07–£0.12/unit, Germany ~€0.27/unit on many parcel sizes).
- Prefer inbound arrivals in Jan–Sep; clear slow movers before they age into the 241-day window ahead of peak.

## Japan (JP) Monthly Storage Fees (2026)

Calculated on daily average volume. Formula roughly: unit rate × (volume cm³ / 1000) × (days stored / days in month). Rates include 10% consumption tax in many published figures.

Approximate 2026 rates (per liter / 1000 cm³):

| Category                          | Size          | Jan–Sep     | Oct–Dec (Peak) | Multiplier |
|-----------------------------------|---------------|-------------|----------------|------------|
| General (non-apparel)             | Small/Standard| ¥5.676     | ¥10.087       | ×1.78     |
| General                           | Large/Special | ¥3.278     | ¥6.984        | ×2.13     |
| Apparel / Shoes / Bags            | All           | ¥3.1       | ¥5.5          | ×1.77     |

Peak season (Oct–Dec) roughly 1.7–2.1× higher. Clear slow movers by September when possible.

### Japan Aged Inventory Surcharge

Starts at **271 days**. Charged per 10 cm × 10 cm × 10 cm (1 liter) volume. From April 15, 2026 a minimum ¥20 per unit applies for items aged 366+ days (media and non-media).

| Age                  | Surcharge (JPY per liter)                     |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| 271–300 days         | ¥16.662                                      |
| 301–330 days         | ¥17.475                                      |
| 331–365 days         | ¥18.085                                      |
| 366+ days            | ¥34.239 or ¥20 / unit (whichever greater)    |

Fees include 10% consumption tax. Rounded to nearest yen. Enable automatic removals for inventory aged 271+ days to control exposure.

### India Long-Term / Aged Inventory Fees

Starts after **5 months** (≈150+ days) — different from most other marketplaces. Assessed monthly.

| Age                              | Fee (higher of the two)                          |
|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 5 to 12 months                   | INR 15 per unit **or** INR 552.5 per cubic foot |
| More than 12 months              | INR 30 per unit **or** INR 1,105 per cubic foot |

This fee is charged **in addition** to the regular monthly storage fee. Inventory age is calculated on FIFO basis. Units that sell or are removed before the next assessment date avoid the charge.

## Planning Notes Across All Sites

- Always convert volume correctly (cu ft vs m³ vs liters/dm³/cm³) and use local currency when calculating cash impact.
- Aged surcharge start thresholds differ by marketplace:
  - US, CA & MX ≈ 181 days
  - UK & EU ≈ 241 days
  - JP, AU, AE & SA ≈ 271 days
  - **IN ≈ 5 months (≈150 days)**
  - **BR, SG, TR & EG ≈ 365 days** (single long-term tier)
- Prefer more frequent smaller shipments over aging inventory.
- Q4 peak storage uplift is significant in many marketplaces — time inbound for Jan–Sep when demand allows.
- Verify every figure in the live Seller Central rate card for your specific marketplace and size tier before final decisions — rates can be updated with notice.
