# Keyword Taxonomy Details & Examples

Prefer tool word-frequency / root analysis (SellerSprite or Sif) to accelerate assignment. Use product context only for final refinement.

## 词性 (Nature) Decision Guide

### 核心产品词 (Core Product)
- The shortest phrase that accurately names the product itself.
- Appears in almost every relevant search.
- Example: “reading pillow”, “dash cam front and rear”, “magnetic eyelashes”.

### 类目大词 (Category / Head)
- Broader parent category. High volume, lower precision.
- Example: “pillow”, “camera”, “eyelashes”, “yoga mat”.
- Usually not the main Title focus for competitive products; good for awareness or SB.

### 属性词 (Attribute)
- Describes a physical or measurable characteristic.
- Color, material, size, technology, design feature.
- Example: “memory foam”, “LED”, “4K”, “waterproof”, “ergonomic”, “black”, “large”.

### 功能卖点词 (Function / Benefit)
- What the product *does* or the outcome for the user.
- Example: “pain relief”, “noise cancelling”, “quick dry”, “anti fog”, “orthopedic support”.

### 场景词 (Scene / Use-case)
- Where, when, or in what situation the product is used.
- Example: “for travel”, “bedside”, “office desk”, “camping”, “gym”, “car”.

### 人群词 (Audience)
- Who the product is for.
- Example: “for kids”, “for women”, “for seniors”, “for beginners”, “for men”.

### 规格参数词 (Spec / Parameter)
- Concrete numbers or pack sizes.
- Example: “500ml”, “6mm thick”, “10 pack”, “12 inch”, “2 lens”.

### 长尾修饰词
- Combinations of the above that form longer, more specific phrases.
- Usually lower volume, higher intent.

### Brand-related
- **自有品牌词**: Only own registered brand.
- **竞品品牌词**: Use only in dedicated PPC campaigns (Exact or Product targeting). Never in organic listing fields.

### 关联互补词
- Products commonly bought or used together.
- Example: “pillowcase” for a reading pillow, “mount” for a dash cam.

### 礼品词
- Explicit gift intent.
- Example: “gift for mom”, “birthday gift for her”, “Christmas gift”.

### 否定词
- Terms that attract wrong traffic.
- Common starters: free, cheap, used, broken, repair, DIY, wholesale (unless B2B), competitor names you do not want.

## Volume Tier Calibration Notes

Different categories have very different volume distributions. Always calibrate:

- High-competition mass categories (phone accessories, home textiles): High = ABA < 20–30k
- Niche / specialized categories: High may be ABA < 100k

When only tool estimates (Helium 10 / SellerSprite) are available, treat them as relative ranking inside the list rather than absolute truth.

## Relevance Judgment Heuristic

1. Mentally (or actually) search the keyword on Amazon.
2. Look at the first page of organic results.
3. Ask: “Would a shopper who typed this be happy to buy *my exact product*?”
   - Most results look like mine → 高相关
   - Related category but different main benefit/size/audience → 中相关
   - Mostly different products → 低相关

For bulk tagging, use the product’s main features + top competitor ASINs as the reference set.

## Priority Assignment Logic (quick formula)

Priority Score ≈ Relevance weight (5) + Volume weight (3) + Intent clarity (2)

- P1: Score high + 高相关
- P2: Medium score or good supporting role
- P3: Everything else that still has some value
