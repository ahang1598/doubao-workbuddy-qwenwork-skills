# Contract Review Matrix

## Universal Rules

Always apply these 12 rules before family-specific cards:

| ID | Topic | Required check |
|---|---|---|
| UNI-R01 | Parties and authority | Legal names, capacity, affiliates, guarantors, approvals, and signature authority |
| UNI-R02 | Document hierarchy | Main agreement, orders, schedules, policies, external terms, and conflict priority |
| UNI-R03 | Definitions and references | Defined terms, capitalization, cross-references, schedules, and internal consistency |
| UNI-R04 | Scope and acceptance | Deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, completion standards, testing, cure, and rejection |
| UNI-R05 | Economics | Price, currency, taxes, invoices, disputed amounts, set-off, adjustment, and audit |
| UNI-R06 | Time | Effective date, term, milestones, renewal, notices, cure periods, expiry, and survival |
| UNI-R07 | Risk allocation | Warranties, exclusions, indemnity, defense control, liability caps, carve-outs, and insurance |
| UNI-R08 | Compliance | Licensing, anti-bribery, sanctions, export, competition, consumer, and sector requirements |
| UNI-R09 | Protected interests | Confidentiality, personal data, customer data, background IP, deliverables, and external materials |
| UNI-R10 | Change and discretion | Change rights, approvals, evidence, unilateral discretion, policy updates, and consequences |
| UNI-R11 | Suspension and exit | Suspension, termination, refunds, transition, return, deletion, handover, and survival |
| UNI-R12 | Disputes and boilerplate | Governing law, forum, arbitration, interim relief, service, assignment, waiver, and language |

## Shadow Draft

Before marking the document, construct the contract that should exist at a
functional level. Compare that shadow structure with the received text.
If the user supplied a playbook, use its structure as the primary checklist and
record the preferred position, bottom line, required direction, and approval
owner for each applicable item.

| Area | Review questions |
|---|---|
| Parties and authority | Correct entities, capacity, affiliates, guarantors, signing authority |
| Scope | Deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, change control, acceptance |
| Economics | Price, currency, taxes, invoices, payment conditions, set-off, audit |
| Time | Effective date, milestones, delay, renewal, expiry, survival |
| Functional clauses | Obligations, rights, discretion, representations, warranties, declarations, conditions |
| Risk allocation | Indemnity, liability, exclusions, caps, insurance, security, remedies |
| Protected interests | Confidentiality, data, intellectual property, personnel, compliance |

| Disputes | Governing law, forum, escalation, interim relief, service |
| Boilerplate | Assignment, amendment, waiver, notices, force majeure, severability, entire agreement, counterparts, language |

## Structural Hard Check

Review these before ordinary clause analysis:

| Parameter | Required comparison |
|---|---|
| Territory and field | Preferred scope, bottom line, actual scope, exclusions |
| Exclusivity | Beneficiary, burdened party, duration, carve-outs, performance conditions |
| Parties and roles | Correct entity, affiliate reach, guarantor, beneficiary, direction |
| Rights direction | Grantor, recipient, control, consent, release, option, indemnity |
| Term and exit | Initial term, renewal, termination, transition, survival |
| Economics | Price, tax, audit, security, set-off, currency, adjustment |
| Control | Approval rights, change control, prosecution, enforcement, settlement |

Use only `conforms`, `deviates`, `below bottom line`, `absent`, `reversed`, or
`not specified`. Escalate reversed structural terms first.

## Bidirectional Review

- Forward: identify harmful or inconsistent language that is present.
- Reverse: test every applicable playbook or shadow-matrix item for missing,
  diluted, below-bottom-line, or reversed protection.
- Do not infer that an omitted protection is acceptable merely because the
  received contract is internally consistent.
- Report descriptive counts by status; do not issue a contract safety score.

## Rights Symmetry

For each material right, compare beneficiary, burdened party, trigger,
qualifier, exception, remedy, duration, and survival. A one-way right may be
appropriate, but it must be intentional and consistent with the transaction.

## Intellectual Property Layers

For pre-existing IP, deliverables, improvements, and joint developments,
separately review:

1. ownership and license scope;
2. prosecution, maintenance, control, cost, and cooperation; and
3. enforcement, settlement control, recoveries, standing, and cooperation.

## Ambiguity Scan

1. Semantic: one word has multiple plausible meanings.
2. Syntactic: modifiers, negatives, conjunctions, or punctuation attach in more than one way.
3. Contextual: definitions, priority clauses, exceptions, schedules, or external documents conflict.

Distinguish deliberate flexibility from ambiguity. A flexible standard is
acceptable only when the decision-maker, factors, evidence, and consequence are
clear enough for the intended transaction.

## Cascade Scan

After every material edit, search the whole document for:

- the same defined term and synonyms;
- related representations, warranties, obligations, conditions, and remedies;
- cross-references and schedule references;
- dates, periods, formulas, thresholds, and currency;
- termination, survival, indemnity, insurance, and liability carve-outs;
- bilingual counterparts and language priority.

## Risk Rating

- High: could invalidate the bargain, create uncapped or material exposure, block performance, transfer core rights, or violate mandatory law.
- Medium: meaningful allocation, enforceability, operational, or negotiation issue with manageable correction.
- Low: clarity, process, drafting, or limited commercial issue unlikely to determine the transaction alone.

Rate by impact multiplied by likelihood and state the assumptions used.
