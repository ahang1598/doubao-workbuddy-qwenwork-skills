# Playbook Calibration And Coverage

## Applicable Instruction Set

Use only a playbook, template, policy, review guide, or instruction that the
user supplied or clearly designated for the matter. Do not infer that an
unrelated workspace file governs the transaction.

Extract each applicable requirement into:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Section | Playbook or clause-matrix topic |
| Requirement | Required legal or commercial result |
| Preferred position | Target outcome |
| Bottom line | Lowest approved outcome |
| Direction | Which party grants, receives, controls, or bears the item |
| Priority | Escalation order |
| Approval owner | Person or function authorized to accept deviation |
| Basis label | `[用规]`, `[要点]`, `[法规]`, or `[惯例]` |

Mandatory law overrides a conflicting instruction. Otherwise apply lawful user
rules, stated matter requirements, verified law, and industry practice in that
order.

## Structural Parameters

Hard-check these before clause-level review:

1. Territory.
2. Field, product, service, or permitted-use scope.
3. Exclusivity and its exceptions.
4. Parties, affiliates, beneficiaries, and role direction.
5. Direction and breadth of licenses, releases, indemnities, options, and control rights.
6. Term, renewal, milestones, and exit rights.
7. Economics, tax allocation, audit, security, and payment conditions.
8. Ownership, decision rights, consent rights, and change control.

Use one of these statuses:

- `conforms`: meets the preferred position.
- `deviates`: differs from preference but remains above the bottom line.
- `below bottom line`: requires escalation or correction.
- `absent`: an applicable protection or term is missing.
- `reversed`: the beneficiary, burden, or direction is opposite to the required position.
- `not specified`: no approved position was supplied.

Address `reversed`, `absent` must-have, and `below bottom line` items before
ordinary drafting preferences.

## Bidirectional Coverage

Use both directions:

- Forward scan: inspect terms that are present for harm, ambiguity, conflict, or unenforceability.
- Reverse scan: walk every applicable requirement to detect missing, diluted, or reversed protections.

When a playbook exists, use its structure as the checklist. When none exists,
use a contract-type shadow matrix and label the results as general practice,
not as a user rule.

Report:

- applicable requirement count;
- conforms count;
- deviates count;
- below-bottom-line count;
- absent count;
- reversed count;
- not-specified count; and
- unresolved items with approval owners.

Coverage is descriptive. It is not a safety, enforceability, or signing score.

## Rights Symmetry

For each material right, identify:

- beneficiary and burdened party;
- trigger and duration;
- standard, qualifier, and exceptions;
- reciprocal treatment or reason for one-way treatment;
- remedy, cap, release, or termination consequence; and
- linked definitions and survival provisions.

Symmetry does not require identical rights. It requires intentional,
transaction-consistent allocation rather than accidental one-way drafting.

## Intellectual Property Layers

For pre-existing IP, deliverables, improvements, and joint developments, test:

1. Ownership: title, license scope, restrictions, and retained rights.
2. Prosecution or control: filing, maintenance, decisions, costs, and cooperation.
3. Enforcement: monitoring, standing, control, settlement, recoveries, and cooperation.

Reconcile these layers with confidentiality, data rights, warranties,
indemnities, termination, transition, and survival.
