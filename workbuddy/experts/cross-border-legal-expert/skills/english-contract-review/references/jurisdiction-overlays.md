# Jurisdiction Overlays

Apply only the overlays triggered by the governing law, performance location,
party location, regulated activity, data flow, or asset location. Verify
current primary or authoritative law before using `[法规]`.

| ID | Overlay | Review focus |
|---|---|---|
| CN-OV01 | Entity, authority, and approvals | Registration, business scope, authorization chain, seals, state-assets, foreign-investment, and internal approvals |
| CN-OV02 | Mandatory law and public policy | Non-waivable rules, validity restrictions, public policy, and enforceability limits |
| CN-OV03 | Tax, invoice, and cross-border payment | Tax-inclusive price, invoice type, withholding, permanent establishment, foreign exchange, and payment evidence |
| CN-OV04 | Data, personal information, and cybersecurity | Processing roles, sensitive data, important data, security duties, localization, and cross-border transfer |
| CN-OV05 | Workforce and classification | Actual control, compensation, working arrangements, social insurance, dispatch, and contractor classification |
| CN-OV06 | Competition and channel restrictions | Exclusivity, resale restrictions, most-favored terms, tying, platform rules, and information exchange |
| CN-OV07 | IP, technology, and open source | Service inventions, commissioned development, technology import/export, registration, open-source duties, and trade secrets |
| CN-OV08 | Guarantee, registration, and priority | Corporate approvals, guarantee scope, mortgage, pledge, registration, publicity, and enforcement order |
| CN-OV09 | Public procurement and state assets | Procurement method, tender hierarchy, subcontracting, audit, integrity, and state-asset procedures |
| CN-OV10 | Disputes and enforcement | Arbitration institution, seat, interim measures, service, language, and cross-border recognition or enforcement |

For another jurisdiction, create a matter-specific overlay with the same
fields and mark unverified conclusions `待核查`.
