# Seedance Real Selling Video

Seedance 2.5 real-feel selling video skill for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and other North American or European commerce platforms.

## Skill

`seedance-real-selling-video` helps an agent write and optimize AI-generated UGC selling video prompts that look less like AI output and less like polished ads.

It focuses on realistic mobile-shot product videos with natural skin texture, imperfect lived-in scenes, handheld framing, mixed real-world lighting, stable character continuity, and concise 15-25 second selling scripts.

Use this skill when users ask for:

- TikTok / Reels / Shorts selling videos
- North American or European UGC-style product videos
- Seedance 2.5 prompt optimization
- AI video de-AI and de-advertising treatment
- Realistic product demonstration scripts
- Cross-shot character consistency

## Notes

- The skill is designed for prompt and script generation, not direct media rendering.
- For best results, keep selling videos short, usually 15-25 seconds.
- Use real-world imperfections intentionally: mixed light sources, lived-in background clutter, visible skin texture, handheld vertical framing, and concise spoken lines.
