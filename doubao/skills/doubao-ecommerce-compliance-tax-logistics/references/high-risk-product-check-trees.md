# High-Risk Product Check Trees

Use this reference when a product has a high-risk attribute. Add a compact SKU-specific check tree instead of a generic risk paragraph. Do not copy one SKU's evidence status to another SKU without explicit coverage evidence.

Each check tree must end with one of these statuses: `closed`, `open`, `not applicable`, or `needs official or professional confirmation`.

## Bluetooth / Wireless / Connected Devices

Check:

- Radio certification path for each destination market.
- EMC and electrical safety path where applicable.
- Cybersecurity, privacy, app, cloud, data export, or account-linking risk where applicable.
- Module, antenna, enclosure, model name, firmware, and hardware-change coverage.
- Label, manual, Declaration of Conformity, responsible person/importer, and traceability coverage.
- Platform category or backend upload requirements.

Do not assume a module certificate covers the finished SKU after antenna, enclosure, layout, firmware, model, or label changes.

## Battery / Power Bank / Rechargeable Products

Check:

- Battery chemistry, cell/model, Wh, quantity, installed/packed/separate status, and SKU match.
- UN number, packing instruction, section, cargo/passenger aircraft restrictions, SoC limits where applicable, and carrier acceptance.
- UN38.3 test summary, SDS/MSDS, battery specification, DG declaration, labels/marks, and packaging instructions.
- Platform dangerous goods review, FBA/warehouse acceptance, return/refurbish/disposal process, and recall traceability.
- Whether battery evidence covers the exact production cell, supplier, SKU, and batch.

Do not treat `<100Wh` as automatic permission to ship by any route. Carrier, platform, packing instruction, and evidence gates still apply.

## Skin-Contact / Health / Beauty / Massage Products

Check:

- Skin-contact material evidence for silicone, TPU, plastic, metal, coating, adhesive, textile, or other contact surfaces.
- Chemical restrictions, allergen/irritation risk, warning language, cleaning instructions, and after-sales complaint handling.
- Medical, therapeutic, pain-relief, rehabilitation, blood-circulation, FDA/CE-approved, or disease-related claims.
- Device classification and platform category risk if the product makes health or treatment claims.
- Recall, batch traceability, hygiene returns, and non-resale/disposal process.

Keep ordinary wellness/comfort claims separate from regulated medical or therapeutic claims.

## Children's Products / Toys

Check:

- Age grading, intended user, foreseeable child use, small parts, choking, battery access, cords, magnets, and button-cell risks.
- Applicable safety standard, test report, certificate issuer, importer/manufacturer obligations, and tracking label.
- Art, character, brand, likeness, and licensing evidence.
- Platform category approval, product images, listing text, and packaging claims.

Do not treat supplier invoices, competitor listings, or market availability as product safety or IP authorization evidence.

## Electrical / Electronic Products

Check:

- Voltage/input, power supply, adapter inclusion, charging interface, wireless function, moving parts, heat, and battery status.
- EMC/LVD/RED or local equivalent applicability based on product attributes, not generic electronics assumptions.
- RoHS, WEEE, battery EPR, packaging EPR, responsible person/economic operator, local language manual, warning labels, and traceability.
- Certificate, test report, DoC, model, factory, component, and batch coverage.

Do not treat a certificate alone as a complete technical file or manufacturer Declaration of Conformity.

## EU Cosmetics

Check:

- Product category and claims boundary: cosmetic vs medicine, medical device, biocide, or drug-like claim. Claims such as acne treatment, whitening, repairing sensitive skin, anti-inflammatory, or therapeutic effects need extra scrutiny.
- Responsible Person, Product Information File, CPSR/safety assessment, CPNP notification, GMP evidence, ingredient/INCI list, allergens, nanomaterials, CMR/restricted substances, batch/lot traceability, and label language.
- Whether supplier documents are EU-relevant: Chinese ingredient list, domestic test report, SDS, or general COA does not replace CPSR, PIF, RP, CPNP, and compliant EU labeling.
- Channel and stock structure: Shopify direct mail, TikTok Shop, marketplace role, EU warehouse/3PL, VAT/OSS/IOSS applicability, import VAT, and platform evidence must be separated.
- Claims and ads: before listing/localization/ads, define allowed claims, prohibited therapeutic/medical claims, required disclaimers, and evidence needed.

Do not state that EU sale is allowed before RP, CPSR/PIF, CPNP, compliant label, claims boundary, and route-specific tax/import evidence are resolved or explicitly marked as pending.

## Food Contact Materials

Check:

- Material and use conditions: silicone/plastic/metal/coating/ink/adhesive, direct food contact, temperature, duration, repeated use, dishwasher/oven/air fryer use, fatty/acidic/aqueous food simulants, and max-use claims.
- EU FCM framework and member-state expectations: Declaration of Compliance where applicable, supporting test report, migration test conditions, sample/model/batch coverage, traceability, GMP, and local language instructions/warnings.
- Do not misuse third-country evidence: FDA, LFGB, supplier “food grade,” or generic third-party reports are evidence clues only; they do not automatically prove EU-wide FCM compliance for the exact SKU and use condition.
- Packaging EPR and marketplace requirements for Germany, France, Poland or other target countries, separated from product FCM compliance.
- IP/design risk: product shape, patented design, brand-like appearance, packaging artwork, and claims such as `FDA certified`, `100% non-toxic`, `absolutely safe`, or similar absolute safety statements.

Do not clear launch or stocking before the exact material, test sample, batch/model coverage, use conditions, label/claims, EPR path, and IP risk are resolved or routed to professional confirmation.

## Food, Cosmetics, Liquids, Powders, Magnets, Chemicals, Or Restricted Materials

Check:

- Ingredient/material evidence, concentration, function, intended use, target user, and local admission category.
- Label language, claims, warnings, responsible party, batch/lot, shelf life, and safety documents.
- SDS, transport classification, leakage/packaging, carrier acceptance, and marketplace restrictions.
- Import permits, product registration, notification, testing, or professional confirmation where applicable.

Do not give a final sell/ship conclusion if ingredients, claims, labels, or admission category are unresolved.
