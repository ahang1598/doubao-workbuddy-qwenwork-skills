# Tax And IP Playbooks

## Tax Workflow

| Step | What to determine | Required fields |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax geography | Ship-from, warehouse location, customer destination, seller entity country |
| 2 | Channel structure | Marketplace, Shopify/independent store, wholesale, B2B/B2C |
| 3 | Marketplace facilitator | Whether Amazon/Etsy/TikTok/etc. collects/remits for this jurisdiction |
| 4 | Nexus / registration trigger | Physical presence, economic thresholds, warehouse/FBA inventory, sales/transaction volume |
| 5 | VAT/GST mechanism | VAT registration, OSS/IOSS, reverse charge, low-value goods tax, import VAT |
| 6 | Product taxability | Product tax code/category, exemptions, shipping taxability |
| 7 | Records and reconciliation | Tax ID, order reports, refunds/voids, platform tax reports, filing owner |

Do not give final tax advice. Output a decision framework and confirmation checklist for accountant/tax advisor or official tax authority.

## EU VAT / OSS / IOSS

| Scenario | Check |
|---|---|
| EU B2C cross-border sales | Destination country VAT, OSS registration path, marketplace role |
| Low-value imported goods | IOSS applicability, import VAT, platform/marketplace responsibility |
| EU B2B | VAT ID validation via VIES, reverse charge possibility, invoice requirements |
| Non-EU seller | Member State of Identification, fiscal representative need, marketplace facilitation |

## US Sales Tax

| Scenario | Check |
|---|---|
| Marketplace sale | Marketplace facilitator collection/remittance and reporting exclusion |
| Independent store | State nexus thresholds, ship-to state, product taxability, shipping taxability |
| FBA or 3PL inventory | Physical nexus or inventory presence signals |
| Refunds | Return/void records to avoid over-remittance |

## IP Workflow

| Step | What to check | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify IP elements | Brand, logo, character, artwork, product shape, packaging, patented utility |
| 2 | Search official databases | USPTO or target-market trademark/patent/design search |
| 3 | Check status and scope | Live/dead, owner, goods/services, class, filing/registration status |
| 4 | Check authorization chain | Brand authorization, distributor invoice, supplier license |
| 5 | Platform risk | Brand Registry, IP complaints, counterfeit/gray-market policies |
| 6 | Decide action | Remove risky terms, request authorization, avoid product, consult attorney |

Never say “no infringement” unless the user supplies professional/legal clearance. Use “risk not resolved” or “lower visible risk based on provided evidence.”
