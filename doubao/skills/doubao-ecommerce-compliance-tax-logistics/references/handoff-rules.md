# Handoff Rules

## Stay In This Skill

- Can this product be sold, imported, exported, cleared, fulfilled, or stored?
- Does this product need certification, labeling, safety docs, SDS, approval, or authorization?
- What tax/VAT/GST/Sales Tax/Nexus/OSS/IOSS questions must be verified?
- What HS/tariff/customs documents and DDP/DDU setup are needed?
- Which fulfillment route is viable after compliance and customs checks?

## Hand Off

| User need | Target skill | What to pass |
|---|---|---|
| Market demand, competitor landscape, product selection, profit opportunity | `doubao-product-research` | Risk blockers, landed-cost fields, restricted categories |
| Listing copy, PDP, images, keywords, local language rewrite | `doubao-listing-localization` | Allowed claims, prohibited claims, required labels/certifications, IP caveats |
| Ads, PPC, ACOS/TACOS, bids, campaign structure, offsite growth | Outside this skill; do not route to a named skill unless a current ads/growth skill is available | Product eligibility and claims boundaries |
| TikTok scripts, UGC, creator briefs, short videos | `doubao-cross-border-growth-content` | Prohibited claims, content restrictions, required disclaimers |

## Handoff Note Ideas

Use concise handoff notes in your own words. These are reminders of what to pass along, not examples to copy:

- “合规/税务/清关边界如下：...。在这些边界内，可以交给 Listing Skill 改写标题和卖点。”
- “当前 P1 风险是 HS/认证未确认，建议先补资料；广告和备货要等验证后再推进。”
- “这个问题主要是选品机会判断，本 Skill 只保留禁售、IP、关税和履约字段作为门禁。”
