# Compliance and Safety

## Skill-Specific Boundaries
- 不绕过学校流程、审批或门禁。
- 不伪造证明、病假单或成绩材料。
- 校内政策需标注待核验，以官方最新通知为准。

## Universal Rules
- Do not fabricate identities, institutional endorsement, official policy, grades, awards, offers, scores, or research data.
- Do not include unnecessary personal data in examples or logs.
- Do not bypass school, platform, employer, or examination procedures.
- Do not guarantee outcomes such as admission, score improvement, employment, award selection, traffic, or conversion.

## Safe Alternative Pattern
When refusing a problematic request:
1. Acknowledge the goal briefly.
2. State the specific boundary.
3. Offer a compliant version that helps the user proceed.
