# Content Playbooks

Use these playbooks to turn product facts into platform-native content.

## Angle Library

| Angle | Use when | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Problem / solution | buyer has a clear pain point | show the pain visually, then product use |
| Demo / tutorial | product benefit is best seen in action | keep steps simple and factual |
| Comparison | product differs by feature, size, material, convenience | compare categories or old way/new way; avoid unsupported competitor claims |
| Unboxing / first impression | packaging, gift, visual appeal matter | show what is included; do not add unreceived accessories |
| Review / testimonial | user has real reviews or creator feedback | quote only provided reviews; no fabricated social proof |
| Gift / occasion | product fits holidays or recipients | localize occasion and recipient |
| Objection handling | buyer worries about size, quality, setup, fit, shipping | answer with evidence and visual proof |
| Trend adaptation | platform trend fits product use | adapt structure, not competitor wording or copyrighted elements |

## Hook Patterns

| Hook type | Formula |
|---|---|
| Pain | “Still dealing with [problem]?” |
| Result | “Here is how [product] makes [task] easier.” |
| Curiosity | “I did not expect this small [product] to solve [use case].” |
| Comparison | “Old way vs. this [product] for [scenario].” |
| Gift | “A practical gift for [recipient] who [situation].” |
| Proof | “Watch what happens when [verified demo].” |

## Content Matrix

| Buyer stage | Content job | Best formats |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | make buyer stop and recognize need | short video hook, lifestyle scene, trend adaptation |
| Consideration | explain why this product fits | demo, comparison, tutorial, FAQ |
| Decision | reduce risk | review proof, shipping/returns if verified, size/detail image, creator testimonial |
| Retention | increase usage and repeat purchase | tips, care guide, accessories, user-generated examples |

## Creative Testing

Test one major variable at a time:

- hook;
- first frame;
- product scene;
- creator persona;
- proof type;
- offer framing;
- CTA;
- subtitle language;
- image layout;
- thumbnail/cover.

Track outputs by platform, country, product, angle, creator, format, and claim set.
