# Localization And Market Lenses

Localization is not translation. Adapt content to local language, units, scenes, values, and platform site expectations.

## Required Localization Fields

| Field | Check |
|---|---|
| Country/site | US, UK, MY, VN, MX, EU, JP, etc.; platform site rules may differ |
| Language | primary language, dialect, bilingual subtitles, formality, slang risk |
| Units | cm/inch, kg/lb, ml/oz, Celsius/Fahrenheit, voltage, plug type, clothing size |
| Money/price anchor | local currency, value framing, discount wording, affordability assumptions |
| Scene | apartment size, climate, commute, kitchen/bathroom norms, gifting occasions, religious/cultural context |
| People representation | age, body, skin tone, family structure, gender expression, occupation, sensitive stereotypes |
| Holidays/events | local shopping calendar, religious holidays, gift seasons, school/back-to-work timing |
| Claims culture | local sensitivity around body image, medical claims, sustainability, safety, children, politics, religion |

## Market Lens Examples

| Market lens | Content implications |
|---|---|
| Malaysia TikTok Shop | May need Malay/English or local bilingual framing; consider Ramadan/Eid, modesty, local household scenes, MYR price anchor |
| Vietnam TikTok Shop | Natural Vietnamese phrasing, mobile-first commerce, compact home scenes, local price/value framing |
| UK Amazon gifts | British English, gift recipient and occasion clarity, understated claims, size/fit in metric and local terms |
| US Shopify / Meta | Direct benefit, social proof if verified, shipping/return clarity, strong mobile landing-page match |
| EU marketplace | Claims and consumer information can be stricter; avoid unverified environmental, health, safety, or discount claims |

## Localization Output Pattern

For each content angle, include:

- local buyer situation;
- localized wording or tone;
- unit/price changes;
- visual scene changes;
- cultural or regulatory caution;
- what needs official or user verification.

Describe localization quality honestly: distinguish a machine draft, a bilingual meaning check, and native local review. Use detailed line-by-line checks only when the copy is claim-sensitive, safety-sensitive, or ready for publication.

## Do Not

- Directly translate Chinese promotional phrases like “爆款”, “全网最低”, “神器”, or exaggerated “must buy” language without adapting tone.
- Assume every market reacts to the same urgency, humor, body-image framing, or gift-giving scenario.
- Invent local festivals, laws, or slang if unsure.
