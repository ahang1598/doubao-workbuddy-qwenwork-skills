# Official Source Registry

Use official, first-party, backend, or user-provided evidence before stating platform procedures, eligibility, specifications, or policy requirements.

## Source Record

Every consequential platform rule needs:

| field | requirement |
|---|---|
| `source_id` | stable identifier |
| `url` | direct official page |
| `platform` | TikTok, Amazon, Meta, etc. |
| `market` | exact country/site or `global` |
| `account_path` | Ads Manager, Seller Center, Shop Ads, Store, etc. |
| `supports` | exact claims this source can support |
| `last_checked` | `YYYY-MM-DD` |
| `access_caveat` | login, account-specific, dynamic, or none |

Do not transfer a US Seller Center rule to Malaysia or another market without applicable evidence.

## Unresolved Or Backend-Only Items

Treat these as `待官方/后台确认` until a source record is added:

- TikTok Shop Malaysia product-image and listing requirements;
- the current MY Shop Ads or GMV Max authorization route;
- account-specific Spark Pull, Spark Push, marketing-account or Affiliate Mass Authorization availability;
- Amazon Sponsored Products placement eligibility and account-specific controls;
- current Meta/Instagram boost or Partnership Ads eligibility;
- any review, approval, account status, authorization code, duration, or platform enforcement outcome.

## Evidence Classes

| source class | can support | cannot prove by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Official policy/help | current documented rule for its stated market/path | account eligibility or actual approval |
| Platform backend/export | account state, performance and review result | product truth outside submitted data |
| Product document/spec | product attributes and operating limits | platform approval or market legality |
| User attachment/input | case facts supplied by the user | universal platform rules |
| Public listing | visible price, rating, images and copy at collection time | certifications, backend state, real included items without confirmation |
| Competitor content | creative inspiration | product facts, policy or performance benchmark |

## Source Discipline

- If the URL, market/path or check date is missing, write a verification item rather than a mandatory rule.
- Do not invent fixed authorization durations, account types, code lengths, menu paths or edit permissions.
- Do not describe content as approved, eligible, compliant or ready based only on a policy page.
- Keep dynamic performance benchmarks out of final decisions unless the user supplied them or an applicable authoritative source and collection date are recorded.
