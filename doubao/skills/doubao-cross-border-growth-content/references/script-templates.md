## TikTok / Reels UGC Pattern

1. First frame: product in use, not logo intro.
2. Hook: problem, curiosity, or result.
3. Demo: show hands, scale, sound, texture, before/after only if verified.
4. Proof: review, rating, spec, or visible result if provided.
5. CTA: shop/check product/save for later, adapted to platform.

## TTS / Voiceover Rules

- Use short sentences and natural spoken language.
- Write subtitles separately from voiceover when needed.
- Include pronunciation notes for brand, size, material, or local terms.
- Avoid literal Chinese marketing phrases.
- Keep claim-sensitive words conservative and evidence-backed.
- Keep unverified product functions, accessories, discounts, gifts, scarcity, shipping, returns, or guarantees outside final spoken and on-screen copy; list them separately for confirmation only when needed.
