# Workflow Playbook

## Scenario Diagnosis
1. Identify the artifact type requested by the user.
2. Extract facts: who, what, when, where, why, constraints, audience, tone, source materials.
3. Mark missing facts as `待确认` instead of inventing them.
4. Choose the output template and execute the corresponding path.

## Immediate Readability
- Always put the usable speech/answer in the conversation, not only in an attachment or summary.
- Mark `[停顿]`, `[减速]`, `[换页]`, or `[可删]` where useful.
- Include word count, estimated time, fastest cut, and rehearsal focus.

## Scenario Templates
- Defense or award review: qualification boundary, evidence chain, compliance-vs-forbidden table, Q&A that returns judgment authority to the committee.
- Project pitch: hook, conflict, action, evidence, limitation, next step, team contribution, data-boundary statement.
- Campaign: identity sentence, controllable promises, concrete service actions, no opponent attacks, no guaranteed outcomes.
- Club/interview: one positioning sentence, two evidence stories, role-fit explanation, pressure Q&A with honest limits.

## Compliance Replacement Table
Use a replacement table when the draft risks:
- fabricated achievements or unverified awards,
- uncontrollable promises,
- labeling,卖惨, or exposing family/health/financial privacy,
- attacking opponents,
- overstating team contribution or hiding data limitations.

## Templates

### One-Page Plan
- 背景：一句话说明任务和目标。
- 关键信息：对象、时间、地点、负责人、来源。
- 行动方案：3-6 个步骤，每步有产物或验收方式。
- 风险与待确认：只列真实影响执行的内容。

### Script or Copy
- 开头：明确身份、场景和目的。
- 主体：2-3 个证据点或利益点。
- 结尾：行动号召、确认方式或后续安排。

### Checklist/Table
Columns should usually include: item, owner, deadline, source/evidence, status, note.

## Domain-Specific Outputs
- 自我介绍稿
- 竞选演讲稿
- 面试回答
- 答辩 Q&A
- 表达优化建议

## Quality Standard
A strong output is specific enough to use today, but honest about missing facts. It should reduce the user's next action to editing, confirming, or sending.
