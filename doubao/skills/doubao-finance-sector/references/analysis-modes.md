# Analysis Mode Rules

Check all three analysis tiers against actual task-specific implementation and resource read conditions. Reject a manifest that merely declares compliance, unsupported universal chapters, length/keyword-only routing, conflicting tier names, full=>report/files, mutually exclusive orthogonal business axes, or missing inline fallback. Resolve brief/full by judgment dependency, not shared trigger words. Short complex tasks may need full; long background may still be direct. Assess sources, missing-input behavior and explicit output intent independently of tier. Static approval does not establish behavioral coverage or model performance.

Reward answering the actual question with sufficient evidence. Do not reward length, chapter count, unsolicited reports/files, or use of the word full. Grade observable behavior, not self-declared tier labels. Lack of a requested source weakens only the dependent claim.

The machine-readable source is [analysis-mode-contract.json](analysis-mode-contract.json). Business dimensions are independent and may be combined. Missing required dimensions need clarification or explicitly bounded output, never invented defaults. User-requested output format does not by itself increase analysis depth. A tool limitation affects the deliverable, not permission to fabricate results.

- `direct`: Answer one local question with the minimum sufficient evidence and relevant limitations.
- `brief`: Resolve a focused chain of related questions, its drivers, constraints and verification signals.
- `full`: Resolve coupled judgments, cross-object comparisons or scenarios with comparable evidence, alternatives and falsification.
