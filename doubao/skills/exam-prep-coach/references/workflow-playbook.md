# Workflow Playbook

## Scenario Diagnosis
1. Identify the artifact type requested by the user.
2. Extract facts: who, what, when, where, why, constraints, audience, tone, source materials.
3. Mark missing facts as `待确认` instead of inventing them.
4. Choose the output template and execute the corresponding path.

## Realistic Time Budget
- Compute `名义可用时长`: date range × planned daily hours.
- Then subtract known exam days, thesis/opening report, internship, classes, commute, paperwork, rest, and buffer to get `现实可用时长`.
- If the goal is overloaded, mark P0-P3 priorities and explicitly say what to drop, postpone, or maintain.

## Calendar and Adjustment Rules
- For multi-exam or 21/30/60+ day plans, provide a complete day-by-day calendar when feasible. If too long, provide a week-by-week calendar plus a daily task template for weekday/weekend variants.
- Daily tasks need subject, task, minutes, material, completion standard, and复盘 field.
- Detection thresholds should have at least three levels: `优秀`, `达标/及格`, `预警`; each level maps to an action.
- Dynamic adjustment scenarios must include: score not improving, materials or retest/admission disruption, mood or burnout, and time being occupied unexpectedly.

## Safety and Privacy Risk
- For cheating groups, leaked-question claims, or requests to upload ID/admission-ticket/student-card images, refuse unsafe parts and provide safe alternatives.
- If the user already uploaded sensitive documents to a suspicious group, give immediate steps:撤回, screenshot/evidence, report to platform/school, consider police report, replace passwords, monitor identity misuse, and reissue/loss-report documents when applicable.
- Add concrete scoring strategy, such as protecting CET listening, teacher-qualification structured interview, or public-exam data analysis when those are the fastest scoring modules.

## Templates

### One-Page Plan
- 背景：一句话说明任务和目标。
- 关键信息：对象、时间、地点、负责人、来源。
- 行动方案：3-6 个步骤，每步有产物或验收方式。
- 风险与待确认：只列真实影响执行的内容。

### Script or Copy
- 开头：明确身份、场景和目的。
- 主体：2-3 个证据点或利益点。
- 结尾：行动号召、确认方式或后续安排。

### Checklist/Table
Columns should usually include: item, owner, deadline, source/evidence, status, note.

## Domain-Specific Outputs
- 备考日历
- 每日任务
- 错题复盘
- 阶段检测
- 动态调整建议

## Quality Standard
A strong output is specific enough to use today, but honest about missing facts. It should reduce the user's next action to editing, confirming, or sending.
