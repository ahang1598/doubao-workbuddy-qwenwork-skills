# Output Contracts

## Required Artifact Fields
- 备考日历
- 每日任务
- 错题复盘
- 阶段检测
- 动态调整建议
- 名义/现实可用时长评估
- 隐私脱敏和应急步骤（安全风险场景）

## Minimum Quality Bar
- The result must match the user's context and audience.
- The result must include concrete wording, table rows, schedule, or checklist items when applicable.
- The result must not be only a framework unless the user explicitly asks for a framework.
- The result must label unknown facts and assumptions.
- Realism check must separate nominal available hours from realistic available hours after exams, papers, internship, commute, fixed classes, and rest.
- Multi-exam plans must include a complete day-by-day calendar or a clearly bounded weekly calendar plus daily task template.
- Dynamic adjustment must cover score stagnation, material/admission emergencies, mental-state disruption, and time being unexpectedly occupied.
- Strategy notes must state the fastest scoring modules, the protected priority module, and what to cut first when overloaded.

## Final Review Checklist
- Trigger matched: yes/no.
- Required inputs complete or visibly marked.
- Deliverables present.
- Tone fits the audience.
- No fabricated fact.
- No unsafe or non-compliant instruction.
- User can take a next action immediately.
- Realistic hours, full calendar/task template, and dynamic adjustment cases are present.
