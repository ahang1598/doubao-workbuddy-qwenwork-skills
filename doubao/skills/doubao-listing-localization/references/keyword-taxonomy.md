# Keyword Taxonomy

Use this taxonomy to transform raw user text, supplier titles, competitor listings, or search-term exports into a usable keyword map.

## Keyword Types

| Type | Meaning | Where to use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core product term | The product name a buyer would naturally search | Title front, first bullet, tags | Usually 1-3 phrases |
| Category term | Broader category or marketplace taxonomy | Title, attributes, feed category | Useful for discovery but can be too broad |
| Attribute term | Material, size, color, pack count, compatibility, style, power, feature | Title, bullets, attributes, filters | Must match real product facts |
| Use-case term | Where/how the product is used | Bullets, A+, description, image brief | Good for conversion and long-tail matching |
| Audience term | Buyer or recipient | Title when important, bullets, tags | Examples: women, kids, pet owners, office workers |
| Occasion / gift term | Holiday, season, event, gift scenario | Tags, bullets, A+, campaign copy | Verify local cultural fit |
| Problem / pain term | Buyer problem or review pain point | Bullets, description, A+ | Avoid unsupported medical or performance claims |
| Long-tail term | Specific multi-word query | Backend search terms, tags, content modules | Keep natural; avoid repetitive stuffing |
| Backend / tag term | Search-only or marketplace tag field | Backend terms, Etsy tags, feed fields | Do not repeat title excessively |
| Cautious / blocked term | Brand, IP, certification, medical, prohibited, absolute, or unsupported claim | Risk table only | Do not use in final copy unless verified and safe |

## Cleanup Rules

- Remove repeated words unless the platform requires an exact phrase.
- Do not include "hot search", "best", "No. 1", "free shipping", price, coupon, urgency, or unsupported claims in marketplace titles.
- Do not use competitor brand names, celebrity names, movies, anime, sports teams, or protected character terms in final copy unless the user proves authorization.
- Keep title language readable for humans. Search engines and marketplace algorithms reward relevance, but customers still decide whether to click.
- Preserve exact product facts: material, size, voltage, model compatibility, package quantity, and variant must not be invented.

## Localization Rules

- Translate by buyer intent, not word-by-word.
- Convert units and sizes when the target country expects local conventions, but keep the original spec if it matters for compliance or compatibility.
- Adapt gift/holiday terms to the target market.
- Keep local category names and buyer phrases distinct from supplier/manufacturer language.
- If a phrase sounds like a Chinese factory title in English, rewrite it from the buyer's point of view.

## Evidence Strength

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | From Seller Center/export, user product file, official rule, or directly visible PDP fields |
| Medium | From public competitor patterns or repeated marketplace search suggestions |
| Low | From third-party tools, inferred synonyms, or general language knowledge |

When confidence is medium or low, label it and recommend validation.
