# Risk And Handoff

## Risk Checks

| Risk | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Competitor brand / trademark | Nike, Disney, Pokemon, Stanley, LEGO, character names | Do not use in final copy unless authorization is proven; hand off to compliance when in doubt |
| IP / copyrighted character | anime figures, movie names, sports teams, celebrity references | Flag as infringement risk; avoid final listing claims |
| Certification / regulated product | CE, FCC, FDA, UL, ETL, NFPA, child safety, medical devices | Ask for proof; hand off to compliance for listing eligibility |
| Medical / health claims | cures, pain relief, antibacterial, whitening, therapeutic | Require evidence and platform policy check; rewrite cautiously |
| Absolute / unverifiable claims | best, No. 1, guaranteed, hottest, lowest price | Avoid unless verified and platform-safe |
| Price / promotion / availability | free shipping, coupon, today only, in stock | Usually avoid in marketplace title/bullets; use platform promo fields if allowed |
| Contact / external traffic | phone, email, website URL, social handle | Avoid in marketplace listing fields |
| Keyword stuffing | repeated terms, unreadable title, search-term dumps | Convert to keyword map and rewrite naturally |
| Local cultural mismatch | wrong holiday, wrong units, awkward translation | Localize by buyer intent and country/site |
| Live data fabrication | exact search volume, rank, sales, BSR, policy | Give verification path; do not invent |
| Evidence overextension | one child/sample/model test presented as true for a parent family | Record evidence scope; restrict the claim to the covered child/sample or remove it from shared copy |
| Compatibility inference | sole type, fitment, device compatibility, model-year standard inferred from a similar name | Require exact SKU/model/year evidence; block buyer-facing compatibility language until verified |
| Unsupported experience claim | comfortable all day, secure, safe, durable, gentle, ideal for a body location | Require direct evidence for the exact wording or replace with a factual construction/specification |

## Claim Whitelist

Classify every buyer-facing claim before drafting:

| Status | Meaning | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Verified | Direct product evidence supports the wording and its parent/child/model scope | May use in final copy |
| Conditional | Plausible but proof, scope, or exact wording is incomplete | Keep out of final copy; list the needed verification |
| Blocked | Contradicted, prohibited, unauthorized, or unsupported | Remove and explain the risk briefly |

For test reports, certifications, dimensions, weight, materials, packaging, compatibility, and safety/performance claims, record the exact covered SKU/sample/model. A family name or visually similar model is not evidence of shared specifications.

|---|---|---|
| Create title, bullets, description, A+, tags, backend terms | Yes | None |
| Translate/localize existing listing for another country/site | Yes | None |

## Safe Language Patterns

Use:

- "Based on the provided attributes..."
- "If this claim is supported by test/certification documents..."
- "Suggested keyword candidates; verify search volume in Seller Center or keyword tools."
- "Avoid using this competitor brand term in final copy unless authorized."
- "This belongs to ads/compliance/logistics; here is the listing-side preparation."
- "This evidence covers SKU/sample X only; do not use the claim in parent-shared copy until the remaining variants are verified."

Avoid:

- "This keyword has high search volume" without evidence.
- "This product is compliant" without official/legal proof.
- "Use Nike/Pokemon/Disney in your title" without authorization.
- "Guaranteed best seller" or "No. 1".
- "FDA approved" or similar regulated claims unless user provides proof and platform policy allows it.
