# Xiaohongshu travel research

Use this workflow for destinations, itineraries, transport, accommodation areas, booking, food, photo locations, budgets, and travel pitfalls.

## Establish constraints

Infer or obtain the destination, travel dates or season, trip length, group type, pace, budget, transport mode, and priorities. Make explicit assumptions for missing non-critical details.

## Collect useful notes

1. Search inside Xiaohongshu using the destination plus one or two natural constraints, such as trip length, weekend, family, self-drive, transport, or accommodation.
2. Compare multiple notes from different authors and angles when available.
3. Prefer actionable routes with day-by-day order, travel time, transit details, booking requirements, price ranges, alternatives, and specific pitfalls.
4. Use images as evidence when they show routes, maps, menus, queues, viewpoints, or schedules. Do not infer unseen details.
5. Cross-check volatile details such as price, opening hours, reservation rules, queues, and transport. Record the observed date and recommend an official-source check before travel when the detail may change.

## Deliver

Provide an executable route first, followed by transport, accommodation area, reservations, budget, food or photo-location notes, and specific pitfalls with alternatives. Include a compact list of the selected Xiaohongshu notes and direct links. Separate consensus, personal anecdotes, and unresolved conflicts.
