# Xiaohongshu note collection

Use this workflow for product reviews, gift ideas, inspiration, note collection, and content benchmarking.

## Search and shortlist

1. Break the request into the core object plus natural constraints such as audience, scenario, budget, model, skin type, or season.
2. Search with short phrases. Refine the wording over a few bounded rounds if results are noisy.
3. Compare several candidates when available. Do not choose the first note only because its title matches.
4. Filter obvious ads with little substance, title-only matches, copied content without attribution, and notes that provide no evidence or reusable detail.
5. Prefer notes with concrete comparisons, steps, parameters, lists, boundaries, drawbacks, visual evidence, and credible author context. Engagement can be a discovery signal, not proof of truth.

## Extract and cross-check

- Expand the note and read the visible body, images, captions, and relevant comments.
- Capture the author's main conclusion, supporting details, applicable audience, limitations, pitfalls, and what the images substantiate.
- Cross-check consequential claims such as price, product specification, effect, or safety across multiple independent notes when possible. Label claims that remain anecdotal or unverified.
- Respect copyright: summarize and synthesize; do not reproduce a note at length.

## Deliver

Default to a compact set of strong notes. For each, include title, author, direct link, retrieval date, one- or two-sentence summary, reusable points, limitations, and why it was selected. Then synthesize agreement, disagreement, and a practical next step.

If the user asks to mark selected notes with likes or favorites, switch to [interact.md](interact.md) before changing account state.
